# On Chain Munis ERP (USVI) v1

**Concept demo** of how Tyler Munis modules for the Government of the Virgin Islands could leave a Solana audit trail.

Modules in the picture: general ledger, budget, purchasing and accounts payable, payroll, grants, and fixed assets. Inventory is named as a later phase and is not shown as a register.

It is **not** a wallet, a payment system, or a live feed. It never moves funds.

Two maintenance figures are from public Post Audit writeups. Budget bars, ledger batches, the sample purchase order, grants, and assets are made up. The signed Tyler agreement is not public. This page does not include a contract file.

This is a later phase concept, not the first pilot, and not a proposal of record.

## Ground rules

- The bank and the Department of Finance authorize every payment. Funds always move through a bank.
- Majestic Advising and GotSOL.app never hold, control, or recover keys.
- No personal data on chain. Only hashes, totals, and approval memos.
- Munis stays the system of record in this phase.
- USDC settlement happens only where the bank and the Department of Finance choose it.
- No live transfers.
