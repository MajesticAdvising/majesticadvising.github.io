# Political USDC Donations (Oklahoma) v1

**Concept demo** of how a donor could give USDC to one Oklahoma candidate committee.

The only example is Ryan Skeffington. Office, committee, platform, and volunteer links are placeholders. No office or committee has been filed or announced in this demo.

It is **not** a wallet, a payment system, or legal advice. It never moves funds.

Contributor rows, amounts, bank references, and the acknowledgment email are sample data. Facts about Ryan come from his public LinkedIn profile and posts, viewed 2026-10-30.

This is a later phase concept.

## Ground rules

- A regulated bank partner receives and settles the funds. Funds always move through a bank.
- Majestic Advising and GotSOL.app never hold keys or funds.
- Oklahoma Ethics Commission rules apply to state and local campaigns. FEC rules apply to federal campaigns.
- Every rule on the page is illustrative until Steph (Compliance), counsel, and the bank partner finish the review.
- One sample candidate only. Do not add other candidates or campaign claims.
