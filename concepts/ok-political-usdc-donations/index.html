The idea

Support Ryan Skeffington in USDC

A donor gives USDC to Ryan Skeffington's candidate committee. This is a sample. No committee or office has been filed or announced in this demo. A regulated bank partner receives and settles the funds. The donor gets an emailed contribution acknowledgment with the disclosure fields that campaign finance rules typically require, and the committee gets a ledger ready for its reports.
