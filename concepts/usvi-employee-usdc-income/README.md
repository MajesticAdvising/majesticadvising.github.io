# Employee USDC Income (USVI) v1

**Concept demo** of how Government of the Virgin Islands employees could choose to receive part of their pay in USDC.

It is **not** a wallet, a payment system, or a live feed. It never moves funds.

Roster counts, names, titles, and annual pay are public Division of Personnel (DoP) and OpenGov records, cited on the page. Biweekly figures are annual pay divided by 26, gross, for illustration. No withholding is shown.

This is a later phase concept, not the first pilot. Current pilot plans leave out payroll and federal funds.

## Ground rules

- The bank authorizes every transaction. Circle signs on the bank's instruction.
- Funds always move through the bank.
- GotSOL.app never holds keys and never moves or converts money. It only shows what the bank has already approved.
- Majestic Advising never holds funds or keys.
- USDC is not a bank deposit and is not FDIC insured.
- Dollar pay stays the default. USDC is opt in.
- No live transfers. The page carries a demo only banner on every screen.
