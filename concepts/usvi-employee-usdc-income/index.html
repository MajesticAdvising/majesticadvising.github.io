What it does not do: hold keys, move money, convert money, or act as a bank or money transmitter.

Who approves

Bank and Department of Finance authorize, then Circle signs

Every payment goes through the bank. Nothing moves until people at Finance and the bank approve it.

1. Payroll file
Finance prepares the normal payroll run, including opt in USDC amounts.
2. Finance approves
The Department of Finance approves the batch in its usual controls.
3. Bank authorizes
The bank reviews and authorizes each transaction. Dollars are funded through the bank.
4. Circle signs
Circle signs only on the bank's instruction and sends USDC to employee wallets.
5. Employee sees it
GotSOL.app shows the receipt. It never touches keys or funds.
Control rules shown here
  • GotSOL.app never holds keys.
  • The bank authorizes every transaction.
  • Circle signs on the bank's instruction.
  • Funds always move through the bank.
  • Dollar pay stays the default. USDC is opt in.
  • Majestic Advising never holds funds or keys.
Limits

Assumptions and out of scope

Assumptions

  • Biweekly equals annual divided by 26, gross, for illustration only.
  • Employee names, titles, and salaries are public DoP records as listed Oct 30, 2026.
  • Head counts compare the DoP public listing with OpenGov authorized positions (Mar 28, 2025). They measure different things.
  • A bank partner and a Circle relationship would have to be in place before any pilot.

Out of scope

  • Live transfers of any kind.
  • Tax, pension, and other withholding.
  • Key custody by GotSOL.app.
  • Money transmission or conversion by GotSOL.app.
  • Federal funds. Current pilot plans leave out payroll. This is a concept for a later phase, not the first pilot.

Important

  • USDC is not a bank deposit and is not FDIC insured.
  • Whether any party needs a license depends on the facts. USVI and federal counsel must review before any pilot.
  • No regulator or government endorsement is claimed.
